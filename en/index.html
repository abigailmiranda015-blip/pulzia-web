Your business pulse, in one conversation.

Pulzia is a voice assistant with an avatar that reads your monday.com boards and tells you how things are going: what got done today, what's overdue and which stage each project is in.

Get startedContact us

What it does

Talks with you

Ask “how are we doing?” by voice or text and get answers from your real board data.

Shows clear panels

Today's activity, overdue follow-ups and projects by stage, in panels designed for mobile.

Made yours

Give it any name, add your company name and choose which boards are your business stages.

In your language

Works in the same languages as monday.com: English, Spanish, Portuguese and more.

Internet call mode

Full-screen call mode with no phone numbers and no telephony costs.

Respects permissions

Only sees what each user can already see in monday.com. It doesn't keep copies of your boards.

Get started

  1. Install Pulzia in your monday.com account and add the Pulzia view to a board.
  2. Enter your company name and your assistant's name, and choose the boards that are your stages.
  3. Tap the microphone and ask how your business is doing.

Pulzia is in private beta. Want to try it? Write to soporte@pulzia.app.
